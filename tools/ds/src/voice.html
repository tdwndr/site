Ted WinderOn XD Connect, a design hack

Back in March, we brought every designer across Lloyds together for an internal conference called XD Connect. It was a blast.

[…]

This was not about evangelising or mandating AI. It was about understanding where it helps, where it doesn’t, and what responsible usage looks like in practice.

[…]

It wasn’t all plain sailing. Projectors broke. The WiFi collapsed. AI got switched off.

But this was all about learning, and we learned so much. In under three hours, 351 attendees across 49 teams created more than 400 coded prototypes.

[…]

But the most important outcome had nothing to do with tooling. It was the curiosity, creativity and culture that thrives when amazing people are given the space to come together.

  1. Start with a momentSomething real, then a short line of feeling.
  2. Name the tensionNot this, but that.
  3. Short sentences when it goes wrongRhythm does the work.
  4. Real numbersSpecific, and in context.
  5. The point behind itAnd then stop.

A few habits

  • Credit people by name.“The real heroes of this are the product team…”
  • A light touch, aimed at myself.“With two little ones, I need all the help I can get.”
  • Close in a line.“And that’s what keeps me going.”
  • In an interface, calmer still.Short, useful, and no exclamation marks or emoji, even though I use both now and then in posts.

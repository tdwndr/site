What a traffic light taught me about design

I came back from a school trip with a souvenir: a frosted glass mug featuring the Ampelmännchen, the East German traffic light figure designed by Karl Peglau in 1961.

It wasn’t designed to be iconic. It was designed to solve a problem.

It worked so well that it survived German reunification, and it’s still on the streets of the city today.
